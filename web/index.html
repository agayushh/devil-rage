How to play

Level Devil is a short precision platformer you play in the tab you already have open. You run, you jump, and the stage disagrees with you. A floor can be a rumor. A door can be a trap. A coin can be a fuse. The campaign is a sequence of small rooms, five stages to a door, and the joke is that the obvious path is usually the wrong one.

  • Move with the arrow keys, or A and D.
  • Jump with W, Up, or Space. Hold the key for the full hop.
  • Pause with Esc or P. Retry with R.
  • On a phone or tablet, use the Left, Right, and Jump buttons on the game.
  • Click a menu row, or use the arrow keys and Enter.
  • After four deaths on a stage that allows it, S skips ahead.

Progress stays in this browser. Keys you pick up, doors you open, and the death count are written to local storage on this device. There is no account.

Worlds

Three worlds, then a locked door that only answers after the keys are found. Thirteen keys are hidden in the campaign.

Level Devil

Every floor is a rumor. The doors are Floor, Spikes, Motion, Liars, and Threshold. This is the introduction: walk, then don't trust the walk.

Level Devil-er

The rules walked out. Burn, Ballistics, and House Rules add guns, bombs, and buttons that confess more than they should.

Level Devil-est

Bring someone to die with. Company and Glitch put a second body in the room. Your shadow is not on your side.

The true door

It stays locked until the keys are collected. The settings menu knows a rhythm in the arrow keys. That is the only hint the game is willing to give in writing.

Questions

Is Level Devil free to play?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download, account, or payment.

How do I control Level Devil?

Arrow keys or A and D move. W, Up, or Space jumps. Esc pauses. R retries. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

Does progress save?

Yes. Progress, deaths, and keys are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data resets the game.

What kind of game is Level Devil?

A short precision platformer that lies to you. Floors disappear, doors are traps, and the safe path is usually the one that looks wrong.

Is this the original Level Devil?

No. This is an original homage with its own campaign. It is not affiliated with Unept.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The page has on-screen buttons for touch, and keyboard controls for a computer.
